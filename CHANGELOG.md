# Changelog

**English** | [Deutsch](#deutsch)

## v1.1 – 2026-11-03

- New: **FM sound.** OPTIONEN has a new row TON (ORIG. / FM). FM is the sound of the Japanese Master System (YM2413) and switches at once; the setting is stored in battery RAM.
- The patch contains the FM sound driver of the Japanese version; only the English ROM is needed.
- The header region of the patched ROM is now Japan, so that emulators such as ares switch the FM chip on.
- The options window got one more row.

## v1.0 – 2026-10-30

First release.

- All dialogue, narration, system and battle messages, menus, title screen, name entry and credits headings in German; text fully proofread.
- Umlauts Ä/Ö/Ü added to the font; ROM expanded to 1 MB.
- Items, weapons, armour, enemies and spells keep their original English names.
- Quality-of-life features: OPTIONEN (EXP/Meseta 1x/2x/4x, faster walking incl. dungeons, random encounters on/off), battle macros, battle turbo (hold button 1).
